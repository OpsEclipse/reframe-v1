# Future Features

This is a lightweight backlog [a running list of ideas to consider later].
It is not a promise to build everything here.

## High Priority

### Save Reflection History

Let users review reflections they have already completed.

Why it matters:

- A reflection should feel like part of a longer story.
- Users can see how their thinking changes over time.
- The app can reconnect a current reflection to past responses.

### Search Old Entries

Add search across uploaded journal entries.

Why it matters:

- Users can find a memory, person, place, or feeling quickly.
- The archive becomes useful outside the guided reflection flow.
- It gives users more control over their own material.

### Reflection Quality Feedback

Let users mark a reflection as helpful, not helpful, too intense, or off base.

Why it matters:

- The app can learn what tone works for the user.
- It gives users a clear way to push back.
- It helps avoid reflections that feel generic or wrong.

## Medium Priority

### Theme Tags

Generate simple tags for entries and reflections, like work, family, grief, change, or confidence.

Why it matters:

- Users can see recurring patterns.
- The app can choose better related entries.
- Tags make the archive easier to browse.

### Entry Timeline View

Show old entries on a simple timeline.

Why it matters:

- Time is important in journaling.
- Users can see clusters, gaps, and seasons.
- It makes the archive feel more alive.

### Manual Entry Upload

Let users add a single journal entry by typing or pasting text.

Why it matters:

- Not every entry comes from a file upload.
- Users can capture fresh thoughts quickly.
- This lowers the barrier to using the app often.

## Low Priority

### Reflection Tone Settings

Let users tune the reflection style.
Examples: gentler, more direct, more practical, more curious.

Why it matters:

- People need different support at different times.
- The app can feel more personal without guessing.

### Private Export

Let users export their entries and reflections.

Why it matters:

- Users should be able to leave with their data.
- This builds trust.
- It supports backup and long-term ownership.

### Archive Health Check

Show whether uploaded entries are indexed [prepared so the app can search and connect them] correctly.

Why it matters:

- Users can understand when the archive is ready.
- Failed uploads become visible and fixable.
- It makes background work less mysterious.

## Later Questions

- Should reflections become searchable too?
- Should users be able to delete reflection sessions?
- Should the app support multiple journals or life chapters?
- Should the app remember tone preferences across sessions?
- Should the archive show sensitive-content controls?
